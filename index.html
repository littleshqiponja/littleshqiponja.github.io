Alfabeti Shqip · The Albanian Alphabet

Shqipja ka 36 shkronja, përfshirë 9 dyshkronjëshat (dh, gj, ll, nj, rr, sh, th, xh, zh). Çdo shkronjë ka një tingull. Prekni «Dëgjo» për ta dëgjuar.

Albanian has 36 letters, including the 9 digraphs (dh, gj, ll, nj, rr, sh, th, xh, zh). One letter, one sound. Tap «Listen» to hear it.

  • A a
    arush · bear
    a as in father
  • B b
    bukë · bread
    b as in ball
  • C c
    cjap · billy goat
    ts as in cats
  • Ç ç
    çadër · umbrella
    ch as in chair
  • D d
    dardhë · pear
    d as in dog
  • Dh dh
    dhëmb · tooth
    th as in this (voiced, buzzy)
  • E e
    elefant · elephant
    e as in bed
  • Ë ë
    ëmbëlsirë · sweet
    uh as in ago (the lazy vowel)
  • F f
    flutur · butterfly
    f as in fun
  • G g
    gotë · glass
    hard g as in go
  • Gj gj
    gjel · rooster
    softer than Xh: middle of tongue on the palate
  • H h
    hënë · moon
    h as in hat
  • I i
    iriq · hedgehog
    ee as in see
  • J j
    jorgan · blanket
    y as in yes (never English j!)
  • K k
    kalë · horse
    k as in kite
  • L l
    lule · flower
    l as in light
  • Ll ll
    llambë · lamp
    a thick L, like ll in full
  • M m
    mollë · apple
    m as in moon
  • N n
    natë · night
    n as in nose
  • Nj nj
    njeri · person
    ny as in canyon (Spanish ñ)
  • O o
    orë · clock
    o as in more
  • P p
    peshk · fish
    p as in pen
  • Q q
    qen · dog
    softer than Ç: middle of tongue on the palate
  • R r
    rosë · duck
    a single tap, like tt in butter
  • Rr rr
    rrush · grapes
    strong rolled rr (make it purr!)
  • S s
    sy · eye
    s as in sun
  • Sh sh
    shtëpi · house
    sh as in shoe
  • T t
    top · ball
    t as in top
  • Th th
    thikë · knife
    th as in think (soft, no buzz)
  • U u
    ujë · water
    oo as in moon
  • V v
    vezë · egg
    v as in van
  • X x
    xixëllonjë · firefly
    dz as in adds
  • Xh xh
    xhaketë · jacket
    j as in jeep (hard English j)
  • Y y
    yll · star
    ee with rounded lips (German ü)
  • Z z
    zog · bird
    z as in zoo
  • Zh zh
    zhapi · lizard
    s as in measure / French bonjour
